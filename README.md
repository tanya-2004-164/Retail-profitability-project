# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, identify profitable products and categories, evaluate store performance, and explore monthly sales trends. The goal is to turn raw sales data into actionable business insights using data analysis and visualization techniques.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales revenue, profit, and profit margins.
* Compare sales and profitability across stores and product categories.
* Identify top-performing products based on total profit.
* Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
* Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
* Build interactive charts and dashboard-style reports.

## Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data exploration, PivotTables, calculations, and charts.
* **SQL (SQLite):** Data querying, aggregation, filtering, and business analysis.
* **Python:** Data analysis using Pandas.
* **Plotly:** Interactive data visualizations.
* **Google Colab:** Cloud-based Python environment.

## Dataset

The project uses a synthetic retail sales dataset containing 3,000 records with fields such as order date, store, region, product, category, quantity, unit price, unit cost, discount percentage, sales revenue, shipping cost, and profit.

*Note: The dataset is created for learning and portfolio purposes and does not represent actual company sales.*

## Project Workflow

1. Explored the dataset and checked data quality in Excel.
2. Created PivotTables to summarize store, category, and product performance.
3. Used SQL to calculate business KPIs and analyze discounts and monthly trends.
4. Used Python and Pandas to prepare data for visualization.
5. Created interactive Plotly charts for sales trends, store performance, category comparisons, and product profitability.
6. Added interactive Region and Category filters to explore selected segments.

## Key Findings

* **Total Revenue:** Approximately ₹7.11 million.
* **Total Profit:** Approximately ₹1.66 million.
* **Overall Profit Margin:** Approximately 23.36%.
* **Highest-Revenue Product:** Study Desk.
* **Highest-Total-Profit Product:** Office Chair.
* **Highest Product Profit Margin:** USB-C Cable, at approximately 52%.
* **Lowest Product Profit Margin:** Storage Shelf, at approximately 6%.

These findings are based on the synthetic dataset used in this project.

## Business Value

The analysis demonstrates how sales data can help businesses monitor profitability, compare store performance, identify high-margin products, and investigate the potential impact of discounting decisions.
